# The data integrator

Since lane L38 (2026-11-19) the integrator is a component of its own, `chris-integrator/` (package
`chris_integrator`), the top of the chris perception stack; roadsense (chris-roadsense) is one of its
sources. Its design: [chris-integrator/docs/DESIGN.md](../chris-integrator/docs/DESIGN.md). What was built and
measured: [lanes/L38-integrator.md](lanes/L38-integrator.md). The earlier, api-internal integrator of lane L37
is described in [lanes/L37-integrator.md](lanes/L37-integrator.md).
